A workspace for designing and documenting network infrastructure. Devices, ports, connections and VLANs live in one project that can be edited as a diagram or as an inventory. It validates the topology and jumps straight to whatever is wrong, and reconciles devices and networks against CSV exports. Projects work locally without an account, or sync to the cloud with sharing roles, revision history and offline edits.
